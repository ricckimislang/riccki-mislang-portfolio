## Research notes
- The direct sites are the source of truth for their current content and navigation.
- The master plan should translate observed patterns into an original site, rather than copy one portfolio's visual identity.
- Brittany Chiang uses a short role statement, then experience and selected projects with descriptions, technology labels, and résumé access: https://brittanychiang.com/
- Paco Coursey leads with a short positioning statement and concise text-led lists of projects and writing: https://paco.me/
- Lee Robinson shows a compact bio and writing links; this is useful for a content-led alternative but less suited to a new portfolio with little published writing: https://leerob.com/
- Anthony Fu makes contributions and project links part of the introductory copy, with a direct route to a full project list: https://antfu.me/
- Tania Rascia's project index gives each item a one-line description plus appropriate article, demo, and source links: https://www.taniarascia.com/projects/
- Rauno Freiberg's home emphasizes interaction craft and routes to projects/field notes; use as a detail reference, not a template for the main information architecture: https://rauno.me/
- Nielsen Norman Group warns that flat design needs clear clickability cues: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-design-best-practices/
- WCAG 2.2 is the accessibility target: https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/
- Astro supports file-based pages and static content; for only one or two case studies, individual pages may be simpler than a content collection: https://docs.astro.build/en/basics/astro-pages/ and https://v6.docs.astro.build/en/guides/content-collections/

## User constraints
- Equal emphasis on hiring and freelance discovery.
- One or two finished projects available at launch.
- Visual preference: flat or minimalist.
- User selected a monochrome visual style; keep the portfolio interface to black, white, and neutral grays.
- User requested both Light and Dark modes. Proposed behavior: follow device setting until explicit selection, then remember the chosen mode.
- User requested five main areas: Profile (achievements and contact), Projects, Experience, Certifications, and GitHub.
- User selected a desktop sidebar instead of a horizontal header and a centered main content layout.
- User requested a personal image holder in the hero. The revised concept places a 4:5 portrait beside the introduction and moves achievements below.
- User requested a narrower centered content area, fewer words in the hero, and a smaller portrait holder.
- User supplied a reference image for a name-led, short first-person profile. The user prefers this to a job-position headline; the reference's personal claims are not copied.
- Proposed a single sparse dot-and-connector background motif recolored for light/dark themes; generated matching light and dark concept images. Keep reading areas plain and contrast independent of the pattern.
- User requested a vignette on the background. Updated both concept images to fade the decorative motif out before the centered reading column, without darkening the underlying page.
